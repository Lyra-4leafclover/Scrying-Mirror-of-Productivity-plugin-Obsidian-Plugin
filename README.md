# ✧ Scrying Mirror of Productivity ✧

[![Obsidian Plugin](https://img.shields.io/badge/Obsidian-Plugin-purple?style=flat&logo=obsidian)](https://obsidian.md)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-blue.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

> **"Speak to the mirror. It remembers."**  
> An all-in-one productivity suite and luxury workspace for Obsidian: Flow-State Pomodoro Chrono, Directives Task Matrix with Unlimited Nested Subtasks, Multi-Timescale Telemetry & 365-Day Heatmap, Offline Study Logger, Lecture Theater, and Liquid Writings Journal with deep 2-way Markdown vault sync.

---

## ✨ Features

- **✦ Directives Matrix with Unlimited Nested Subtasks**:
  - Add high-leverage tasks with priority levels (`CRITICAL`, `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`) and Pomodoro targets (`🍅`).
  - Break tasks into unlimited micro-steps with collapsible subtask drawers and individual subtask completion tracking.
  - Direct 2-way sync with standard indented Markdown in `ScryingMirror/Tasks.md`.
- **⚡ Focus Flow Chrono**:
  - Circular SVG Pomodoro timer with Focus (25m), Short Break (5m), and Long Break (15m) modes.
  - Connect any parent directive or subtask directly to the timer using the **`⚡ FOCUS`** button.
- **⏱️ Offline & Untracked Study Logger**:
  - Forgot to turn on the timer during a study session? Log past hours/minutes with category tags and subjects into your vault telemetry.
- **📊 Multi-Timescale Telemetry & Analytics**:
  - **Day**: 24-Hour hourly focus distribution bar chart + logged session history.
  - **Week**: 7-Day velocity bar chart with task completion badges.
  - **Month**: Full calendar matrix with intensity resonance and daily metrics.
  - **Year**: 365-Day (52-week) GitHub-style contribution heatmap.
- **🎥 Lecture Theater & Scratchpad**:
  - Side-by-side YouTube study stream with auto-saving notes synced to `ScryingMirror/Lectures/`.
- **🖋️ Liquid Writings Studio**:
  - Personal engineering and reflection logs saved directly as Markdown notes with YAML frontmatter in `ScryingMirror/Journal/`.
- **🎨 Native Theme Integration**:
  - 100% Obsidian CSS variable inheritance (`--background-primary`, `--interactive-accent`, `--text-normal`, etc.). Looks seamless in Light & Dark modes.

## 📦 Installation

### From Obsidian Community Plugins (Official Directory)
1. Open Obsidian **Settings** ➔ **Community plugins**.
2. Make sure **Restricted mode** is turned **OFF**.
3. Click **Browse** and search for **`Scrying Mirror of Productivity`**.
4. Click **Install**, then click **Enable**.

### Manual Installation
1. Download `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css` from the latest [GitHub Release](https://github.com/Lyra-4leafclover/obsidian-scrying-mirror/releases).
2. Place all 3 files in your vault folder under `.obsidian/plugins/obsidian-scrying-mirror/`.
3. In Obsidian, go to **Settings** ➔ **Community plugins** ➔ toggle **Scrying Mirror of Productivity** to **ON**.
